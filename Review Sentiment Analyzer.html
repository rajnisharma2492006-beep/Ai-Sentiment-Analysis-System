About the model

92.1%accuracy on unseen sentences (3,925 test reviews)
0.922macro F1 on that test set
0.984macro ROC AUC, one-vs-rest
75.6%accuracy on 90 hand-written reviews

Logistic Regression on TF-IDF features (single words and word pairs), chosen with grid search and sentence-grouped cross-validation (macro F1 0.880 ± 0.027). The training data holds only about 70 distinct sentences, so everyday wording it has not seen is classified less reliably: on my hand-written reviews roughly 59% of the words were unknown to the model.
